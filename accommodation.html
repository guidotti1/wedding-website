Getting here

Recommended airport

CMH · Columbus, Ohio

John Glenn Columbus International Airport

We recommend flying into John Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH), the main airport serving Columbus. It’s about 6 miles from downtown, with taxi and rideshare options for getting to your hotel and around the city.
